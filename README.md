# samce

a tiny, beautiful, no-config text editor.

> **Status:** early days. Under development, nothing to install yet.

## Goals

- **Tiny.** Small codebase, no dependencies, starts instantly.
- **Beautiful.** Nice themes and a clean screen, with nothing but your text.
- **No config.** Open it and type. It works out of the box.

## Not goals

- No plugins
- No tabs
- No splits
- No sidebar
- No LSP or autocomplete
